# History & Search

Open WebUI provides a powerful system for managing and navigating your previous conversations. Whether you are looking for a specific snippet of code from last month or trying to organize hundreds of chats, the history and search features help you find your data quickly.

## Chat History Sidebar

All your conversations are automatically saved in the **Sidebar**.

![Searching chat history](/images/features/chat/history-search.png)

*   **Persistence**: Chats are saved to the internal database (`webui.db`) and are available across all your devices.
*   **Organization**: Chats are grouped by time period (Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 Days, etc.).
*   **Hover Preview**: Resting the pointer on a chat opens a small card with its most recent messages. Switch it off with **Chat Hover Previews** in **Settings > Interface > Chat**.
*   **Unread Indicator**: Chats with new activity show an unread dot in the sidebar until you open them. A chat's three-dot menu has **Mark as unread** to put the dot back; a folder's menu, and the **Chats** section header's own menu, both have **Mark all as read** to clear a folder or your whole chat list in one go.
*   **Renaming**: Titles are automatically generated by a task model, but you can rename any chat from the pencil icon next to its title in the sidebar. Turn automatic titles off for yourself with **Title Auto-Generation** in **Settings > Interface**.
*   **Archivial**: You can **Archive** chats to remove them from the main list without deleting them. Archived chats are left out of the search window unless you search with `archived:true`, and stay browsable under **Settings > Archived Chats**. An admin can hide **Archive** under **Visible controls** in **Settings > Interface** (admin section); see [Archived Chats](/features/chat-conversations/data-controls/archived-chats) for what that hides.
*   **Selecting Several Chats**: Shift-click a chat to select a range, then use the bar at the bottom of the sidebar to **Archive** or **Delete** the selection. The delete button only appears with the [Allow Chat Delete](/features/authentication-access/rbac/permissions#3-chat-permissions) permission.

*   **Folder Unread Count**: A folder shows how many chats it holds that are unread or waiting for a tool approval, counting its subfolders too. See [Folders & Projects](/features/chat-conversations/chat-features/conversation-organization#unread-badge).

*   **Opening and Closing**: The sidebar slides in and out. Your chats are loaded once when you open Open WebUI, not again each time you show the sidebar, so hiding it and bringing it back keeps everything you had already scrolled into view instead of dropping you back at the newest chats.

Open WebUI tracks read state per chat and updates it when you open a conversation, so unread indicators stay in sync across sessions. Opening a chat clears its unread mark, and the folder counts, in your other open windows right away.

## Searching Your History

The search window searches everything you can open in one place.

1.  Click **Search** in the sidebar or press `Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K`.
2.  Type your query. The window searches:
    *   **Your own chats**, by title and message content
    *   **Chats in folders shared with you**, including their subfolders
    *   **Chats shared with you** by name or through a group. A chat shared with **Allow replies** opens live. Any other shared chat shows as a **Shared snapshot** and opens its share link.
    *   **Messages in channels and direct messages** you can access
    *   **Notes** you own or that are shared with you
3.  Click a result, or press `Enter`, to open it.

Channel messages only appear when Channels are enabled and you have the **Channels** feature permission. Notes work the same way with Notes and the **Notes** permission. Admins always have both permissions. Archived chats are left out unless you search with `archived:true`.

With an empty query, the window lists **Pinned & recent**: your pinned chats and notes first, then everything else by most recent activity.

### Filtering results

The button next to the search box picks the content type (everything, chats, messages or notes), and the filter button next to it narrows by who created an item and where it lives. Active filters show as chips under the search box, and **Reset** clears them.

You can also type the [filter prefixes](#filter-prefixes) into the search box, together with search words. `tag:none` finds untagged chats, and spaces in tag and folder names are written as underscores (`folder:client_work`). Any prefix limits the results to your own chats. While you type, the window suggests prefixes and values; pick one with the arrow keys and `Enter` or `Tab`.

### Working with results

Each result shows its title, where it lives (a folder, **Personal chat**, **Shared chat**, a channel, **Personal note** or **Shared note**) and a short snippet from the matching message. A chat matched only on its title shows its last message. A channel message shows its sender's name as the title. The words of your query are highlighted in the title and the snippet.

*   **Chats** open at the matching message, switching to the branch that holds it.
*   **Channel messages** open the channel with the message's thread at the matching message.
*   **Notes** open on the matching page with the first match selected.

Press `Ctrl/Cmd + Enter` to show or hide a preview of the selected result next to the list.


### How queries are matched

A query of several words finds every item (or chat) that contains all of them, in any order, whether the words come from the title or from any message. Matching ignores case and works on substrings, and titles that equal or contain your query rank first.

### Filter prefixes

In the search window and the **Reference Chats** picker you can narrow results with filter prefixes, alone or combined with a query:

*   `tag:<name>`, chats with a given tag, or `tag:none` for chats with no tags
*   `folder:<name>`, chats in a folder
*   `pinned:true` or `pinned:false`: only pinned chats, or only chats that are not pinned
*   `archived:true` or `archived:false`: only archived chats, or only chats that are not archived
*   `shared:true` or `shared:false`: only chats with a share link, or only chats without one

A filter in the search window searches only chats you own. Notes, channel messages and shared snapshots have no tags, folders or archive state, so they drop out of the results while a filter is in the query.

For example, `folder:work meeting notes` searches for "meeting notes" within the **work** folder.

## Native Conversation Search (Agentic)

When using a model with **Native Function Calling** enabled (see the [**Central Tool Calling Guide**](/features/extensibility/plugin/tools#tool-calling-modes-default-vs-native)), models can search through your chat history autonomously.

### Available History Tools:
- **`search_chats`**: Text search across chat titles and message content, using the same matching as the **Reference Chats** picker and skipping the chat the model is currently in. Returns matching chat IDs and snippets.
- **`view_chat`**: Reads and returns the message history of a specific chat by ID, cut to the newest messages when [`VIEW_CHAT_MAX_CHARS`](/reference/env-configuration#view_chat_max_chars) is set.
- **`search_users`**: Looks up users by name or email so the model can mention them in a reply. Each mentioned user who can see the chat gets an [inbox](/features/chat-conversations/chat-features/notifications#inbox) notification that opens the chat at that reply.

### Why use native tool calling for Search?
This allows the model to leverage its own previous work. You can ask: *"What was the feedback I got on my last email draft?"* or *"Find the Python script I wrote about image processing two weeks ago."*

The model will search your history, identify the correct chat, read it, and provide you with an answer or the code you were looking for, saving you the effort of manual scrolling and copy-pasting.

## Data Management

*   **Export**: You can download individual chats or your entire history as **JSON**, **plain text (.txt)** or **PDF**. The whole history exports as JSON only. There is no Markdown export.
*   **Import**: Drag and drop a JSON chat export into the sidebar to restore it.
*   **Deletion**: You can delete a single chat from its three-dot menu in the sidebar or from the menu at the top of the chat itself, and clear your entire history with **Delete All Chats** in **Settings > Data Controls**.

Deleting needs the [Chat Delete permission](/features/authentication-access/rbac/permissions#3-chat-permissions). Where an administrator has taken it away, none of those delete controls are drawn, so you no longer meet a delete button that refuses when you press it. Archiving is unaffected, and admins are always exempt.
