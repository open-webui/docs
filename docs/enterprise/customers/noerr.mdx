# How Law Firm Noerr turned an eight-week Open WebUI rollout into 600+ active users in week one

![Noerr](/images/enterprise/customers/noerr/hero.png)

## Overview

:::info
Noerr rolled out its **Azure-hosted AI workspace** in **eight weeks**, with **more than 600 employees** using it in the **first seven days**.
:::

### At a Glance

- **Industry**: Legal services
- **Deployment**: Noerr’s own Azure subscription; EU-only data residency
- **Models**: Azure OpenAI GPT-6-Luna and GPT-6.1-Sol, AWS Anthropic Opus-5.5 and Sonnet-5
- **Time-to-deploy**: 8 weeks from project start to rollout
- **Usage**: More than 600 employees active in the first seven days
- **Daily activity**: Daily active usage stabilized at 60-70% of licensed seats in the first two weeks

## About Noerr

Noerr is a top German law firm with more than 450 advisors across six national and four international offices, including London and New York. The firm covers the full depth and breadth of corporate and business law and supports clients with complex, cross-border matters.

To keep its edge, Noerr is investing in responsible AI that enhances the work of lawyers and support professionals without compromising strict client confidentiality and data protection requirements.

## The Challenge: Modernizing legal AI

Before Open WebUI, Noerr operated its own internal interface for LLMs. As the pace of AI innovation increased, the firm needed:

- A modern interface and faster access to new capabilities and features.
- Deep customization and integration options for legal workflows
- The option to stay with EU-only hosting and full control over data, encryption, and logs

## The Solution: NoerrGPT with Open WebUI in Noerr’s Azure environment

Noerr chose Open WebUI because it could be fully deployed inside the firm’s own Azure subscription while remaining highly customizable and fast to iterate:

- **Compute / orchestration**: Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) with GPU node autoscaling for OCR
- **Storage / DB**: Azure Database for PostgreSQL, encrypted at rest with customer-managed keys
- **Networking**: Private VNet with LiteLLM as the central hub for LLM requests
- **Secrets**: Infisical as the secrets manager
- **CI/CD**: Azure DevOps pipelines with ArgoCD for deployments
- **Monitoring**: Sentry for error logging and Gatus for uptime monitoring
- **Model routing**: Azure- and AWS-hosted models in EU regions only, with manual or automatic model selection
- **RAG**: Hybrid search with Azure reranking model
- **Secure Web Search integration**: Linkup and Noerr’s own sources.
- **Sandboxed Terminals**: Isolated terminals on AKS for agentic code execution.

Security and compliance controls include MFA, device compliance checks, RBAC by groups, enforced EU data residency, moderation/guardrails, DLP policies, and audit exports to SIEM.

## Adoption and Enablement

Noerr ran a focused 2-week beta with 15 data and AI experts at the end of the 8-week development period, then opened the platform to the entire organization.

Written documentation and recorded practice sessions with live demos supported internal use cases.

- Agentic data retrieval from core applications and the trade register
- A pre-populated prompt library for document information extraction for contracts and case files, drafting, analyzing, and modifying clauses.
- Q&A with specialized bots over internal policies and guidelines
- Deterministic data wrangling and plotting
- Fully agentic skill selection and execution
